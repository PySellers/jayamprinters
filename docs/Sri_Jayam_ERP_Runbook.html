01 Roles & who does what

Every screen in this manual is written for whichever role actually uses it day to day. There's no public sign-up — the Admin account above creates every other login from the Users page.

Admin full access

Everything below, plus pricing, user accounts, and the power to cancel or delete records.

Counter front desk

Takes orders at the Billing Counter, manages customers, creates quotations and invoices, records payments.

Production workshop

Updates job card status as work moves through the shop, assigns machines and operators, logs comments.

Accounts back office

Everything Counter can do, plus Purchases, Inventory, and the Cash Ledger.

Full breakdown of exactly what's gated and what isn't: see the permission matrix.

02 Billing Counter — the fast path

The screen to use for a walk-in customer standing at the counter. One customer, one bill, printed and done. Everything on this screen updates live — no page reloads between steps.

  1. Open Billing Counter from the sidebar (or go straight to /billing-counter).
  2. Fill in the customer strip — Name, Mobile, GSTIN if they have one. Leaving Name blank saves the sale as "Walk-in Customer."
  3. Pick the product from the Product field. If it needs specs (paper size, colour, etc.) those fields appear automatically — fill in every one marked with *.
  4. Set the quantity. Rate and Amount appear under the line the moment pricing can be calculated — if you see "select all required options to see the price," something above is still blank.
  5. Add more lines with F1 / "Add Item" if the customer is buying more than one thing in the same visit.
  6. Watch the Net Amount at the bottom — it's the true total including tax, live, before you save anything.
  7. Use the cash buttons (₹50/₹100/₹500/₹1000/₹2000) to build up what the customer handed over in Cash Tendered — Change Due calculates itself.
  8. Pick the print format — defaults to 80mm thermal. Switch to 58mm or A4 if that's what's loaded in the printer right now.
  9. Hit Save & Print (or F8). This creates the customer record (if new), the quotation, the job card, and the invoice in one shot, then opens the invoice as a print-ready PDF in a new tab.
  10. Print it. The PDF opens in the browser's own viewer — use Ctrl+P or its print button and choose whichever printer is connected. The screen has already reset and is ready for the next customer.
Note on the print step

The app hands off a correctly-sized PDF and lets the browser's normal print dialog do the rest — the same way printing any other PDF works. As long as the thermal printer's Windows driver is installed on that counter PC, it'll show up as a normal choice in that dialog. No special printer software needed.

To start over mid-bill

Press Esc or hit "New" — clears everything on screen without saving anything.

03 Dashboard

The landing page after login. Two things live here: a slower "Start New Order" form, and the Live Job Status board.

Start New Order

An older, single-item version of the Billing Counter — useful for a quick phone order where you just need to capture a name and a delivery date without the full counter screen. Submitting takes you to the Quotation screen to fill in the item's specifics, then straight through to an invoice, same as the Billing Counter does.

Live Job Status

Every job card in the shop right now, grouped into three columns:

Pending   In Progress   Delivered

A job overdue against its delivery date gets flagged automatically — this is the board to glance at to see what the shop still owes customers today.

04 Customers

Full record of everyone who's ordered — name, phone, email, address, GSTIN, notes.

You rarely need to visit this page directly — Billing Counter and Quotations both create or search customers inline. Come here to fix a typo in a saved address or GSTIN, or to search by name/phone when you need someone's history.

05 Quotations

The formal version of a bill — used when a customer wants a written quote before committing, or for multi-item corporate/bulk orders that don't fit the counter flow.

Status flow

Draft → Converted  — once converted, its job cards exist and it can't be edited further. A quotation can also be marked Rejected if the customer doesn't go ahead.

Creating one

  1. Pick or search the Customer, and a Tax rate if it's not the default 18% GST.
  2. Add one or more line items — same product/attribute picker as the Billing Counter, just without the live running total shown on screen (the total appears once you save).
  3. Create Quotation. It's saved as a Draft — nothing is billed yet.
  4. When the customer confirms, open it and hit Convert to Job Cards — this is the point of no return: job cards get created and the quotation locks.
  5. From there, Create Invoice turns it into a billable invoice.

06 Job Cards

One job card per line item, automatically created the moment a quotation is converted. This is what Production updates as work physically moves through the shop.

Click a status chip on the Job Cards list to change it directly. Use the edit icon to assign a Machine, Designer, or Operator, set a Priority (Low/Medium/High/Urgent), or add a dated comment to the job's running log — separate from the single Notes field, useful for "waiting on customer artwork" style updates over time.

07 Invoices & Payments

Generated from a converted quotation. Pricing is snapshotted at that moment, so a later change to the price list never alters a bill that's already gone out.

Payment status

Unpaid Partially Paid Paid  — set automatically from the payments recorded against it, never edited by hand.

Recording a payment

Open the invoice → Record Payment → amount, method (cash / upi / card / credit / bank_transfer), and an optional reference number. Partial payments are fine — record as many as needed until the balance hits zero.

Printing / re-printing

Print or Download on any invoice, any time — pick A4, 58mm thermal, or 80mm thermal. Re-printing a delivered order's invoice works exactly the same way as the first time.

08 Products & Pricing Admin

This is the one section that determines whether the rest of the app quotes correct prices — everything else just reads from what's set up here.

The three pricing types

Fixed
One flat price per unit, no options to pick — e.g. a rubber stamp of a specific size.
Matrix
Price depends on which attribute options are picked (paper size, colour, sides...) crossed with a quantity band — e.g. photocopying.
Per Area
Rate × square footage — e.g. a banner sized on the spot.

Setting up a Matrix product

  1. Under Pricing Setup, add the Attributes that actually change the price for this category (e.g. Paper Size, Colour) — mark the ones that must always be picked as required.
  2. Add the Quantity Slabs — the quantity bands the price is banded by (e.g. 1–50, 51–100...).
  3. Add any Extra Charges — opt-in add-ons like binding or lamination that don't need their own attribute (flat, per-unit, per-sqft, or percentage).
  4. Go to the product's Price Matrix page and fill in a rate for every combination that's actually sellable. Use the Bulk Grid view instead of the one-at-a-time dialog for anything with more than ~30 combinations.
Only model what actually changes the price

Every attribute you attach multiplies the number of price cells needed. Paper colour name or card model with no cost impact belongs in the free-text Notes field on the line item, not as an attribute — otherwise you're filling in hundreds of price cells for nothing.

09 Purchases & Inventory Admin / Accounts

The stock side of the ledger — what's coming in from vendors, and what's on the shelf.

Recording a purchase

Purchases → New Purchase → pick the Vendor, add a line per Inventory Item with quantity and unit price. Saving it automatically adds that quantity to stock — no separate "receive stock" step.

Inventory

Every item shows its current quantity against its reorder threshold. Anything at or below threshold is flagged Low Stock with a banner at the top of the page. Stock only ever goes up automatically (via a purchase) — use the page directly to correct it by hand for wastage or a stock-take.

10 Cash Ledger Admin / Accounts

A hand-entered daybook of actual cash movement — deliberately separate from invoice payments.

Why this isn't automatic

An invoice payment can be cash, UPI, card, or credit — only real cash-in-hand belongs in this register. Record it here yourself whenever cash physically changes hands, the same way you'd write it in a paper daybook.

Cash in Hand
Receipts − Payments − Bank Deposits
Cash in Bank
Sum of everything deposited

The Cheques tab tracks issued/deposited cheques separately, with a status you update by hand as each one clears — Pending Cleared Bounced.

11 Reports

Sales totals over a date range, with one-click Excel export.

Pick Today / This Week / This Month, or a custom range, to see total sales and invoice count for that window, broken down by day. Excel downloads the same data as a spreadsheet for anything that needs to leave the app — a monthly summary for the owner, numbers for an accountant, etc.

12 Users & Roles Admin

Where every staff login gets created — there's no self-service sign-up by design.

  1. Add User — name, email, a temporary password, and their role.
  2. Give it to them and have them change it the first time they log in.
  3. Toggle Active/Inactive to suspend a login without deleting their history — everything they've created stays attached to their name.

You can't deactivate or delete your own account from this screen — it's the one guardrail that stops an accidental self-lockout.

13 Troubleshooting

"Select all required options to see the price"

One of the fields marked * above it (paper size, colour, etc.) is still blank. Fill in every required field on that line — the price appears the moment it can be calculated.

Save & Print is greyed out

At least one line still can't be priced — usually the same cause as above. If a product genuinely has no rate set up yet for that combination, it needs an entry in Price Matrix before it can be sold.

The invoice PDF opened but nothing printed

The tab opened is the finished document, not a live print job — it still needs Ctrl+P (or the browser's own print icon) to actually send it to a printer. Make sure the thermal printer's Windows driver is installed and selected in that dialog.

A staff member forgot their password

There's no self-service reset. An Admin resets it from the Users page.

A quotation/job card/invoice needs to be cancelled

Deleting a quotation, job card, or invoice, and deleting a payment, are all Admin-only — ask an Admin to remove it rather than trying to work around it.

15 Glossary

Quotation
A priced-but-not-yet-committed order. Becomes job cards once converted.
Job Card
One per line item — the production-tracking record for that specific piece of work.
Invoice
The billable, printable document generated from a converted quotation.
Attribute
A choice that changes price — paper size, colour, print side, etc.
Extra Charge
An optional add-on price (binding, lamination) independent of attributes.
Quantity Slab
A quantity band a price is set for — e.g. 1–50 pages priced differently than 501–1000.
Price Matrix Cell
One specific rate for one exact combination of attributes + quantity slab.
